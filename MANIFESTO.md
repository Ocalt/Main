# Manifesto

## 1. The gap

Technology has made real progress. It has also convinced itself that the progress is further along than it is. Ocalt exists to close that gap: to build the systems, platforms and tools that should exist and do not.

## 2. The name

Ocalt comes from the Latin *occultum*: hidden, not yet revealed. The most consequential ideas in engineering are rarely missing. They are obscured. Ocalt's work is to find them and build them.

## 3. The standard

Every Ocalt product ships under one standard, Engineering Magic. It is the minimum. It is not a tagline.

## 4. The Ocaltsmith

Every engineer at Ocalt holds the designation of Ocaltsmith. It is an identity, not a job title. An Ocaltsmith does not ship mediocre work. An Ocaltsmith builds systems that look inevitable in hindsight and impossible in conception.

## 5. How the pieces fit

OcaltQL is the execution layer. The namespace is the shared environment every product works in. Fabric connects the infrastructure. The applications are views onto the same namespace, not separate silos.

Three design positions follow, and each is recorded in the ledger:

- **One language.** One grammar instead of a separate SDK, API and authentication method per service.
- **One namespace.** Every product runs on the same namespace and credentials model.
- **One control surface.** Anything expressible in OcaltQL can be inspected, configured or orchestrated from a single surface.

## 6. The method

The method is in [handbook/principles.md](handbook/principles.md). In one sentence each:

- The specification is the source of truth.
- Convention is not an argument.
- Uncertainty is surfaced, never papered over.
- No shortcuts. The harder path with the greater reward is the default.
- The product must be robust, powerful and efficient.

## 7. Research

Ocalt conducts original research in computer architecture, novel computation models and AI systems. Write-ups are published at https://ocalt.com/updates. Research prototypes are maintained in the `research` repository. Findings that become ledger entries are linked from [THE-LEDGER](THE-LEDGER/index.md).
