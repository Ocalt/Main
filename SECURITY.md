# Security policy

## Reporting a vulnerability

Do not open a public issue for a vulnerability.

If you believe you have found a vulnerability, or that your account has been accessed by someone else, contact Ocalt through https://ocalt.com/support. State that the report concerns security.

If you suspect your account is compromised, change your password and rotate your keys straight away.

Include: the affected product or repository, the steps to reproduce, the impact, and any proof of concept.

## Never share credentials

Never enter an API key, a Client key or a password at another person's request. Connect a machine only with credentials that belong to you.

## Platform security model

How the platform separates credentials, isolates namespaces and keeps connected machines outbound only is described at https://ocalt.com/security. That page also lists what has not been published yet. See [ROADMAP.md](ROADMAP.md).
