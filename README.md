# Ocalt

**Engineering Magic.**

Ocalt designs and operates a portfolio of technology products across software, platforms, AI, hardware and research. Every product ships under one standard, and that standard is the minimum, not a slogan.

Ocalt is an all-in-one software ecosystem that unifies cloud infrastructure, business workflows and collaborative workspaces under a single, query-driven control layer.

## Mission

The industry has progressed, and has convinced itself it has progressed further than it has. Ocalt exists to close that gap by building the systems, platforms and tools that should exist and do not. The argument is in [MANIFESTO.md](MANIFESTO.md). The claims are recorded in [THE-LEDGER](THE-LEDGER/index.md).
