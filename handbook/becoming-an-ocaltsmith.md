# Ocaltsmiths

Every engineer at Ocalt holds the designation of **Ocaltsmith**. The founder holds it too.

It is an identity, not a job title. An Ocaltsmith does not ship mediocre work, and builds systems that look inevitable in hindsight and impossible in conception.

An Ocaltsmith works to the [principles](principles.md), meets the [definition of done](definition-of-done.md) and is held to the [review standard](review.md).
