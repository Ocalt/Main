# The Ledger

The innovation ledger turns the thesis into citable claims. One entry per stagnated area.

`research` is the evidence. The ledger is the verdict.

## Entry format

See [TEMPLATE.md](TEMPLATE.md). An entry always holds the **counter-design**. It holds the **convention** it answers and the **evidence** where a published source states them. A date of settlement is recorded only where one is documented. A claim without a source is not entered.
