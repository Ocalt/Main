# Code of conduct

This applies to every space run by Ocalt: repositories, issues, pull requests and discussions.

## Standard

- Be direct and professional.
- Criticise the work, not the person.
- Do not harass, threaten or demean anyone.
- Do not publish another person's private information.

## Enforcement

A maintainer may remove content, lock a thread, or block a person who breaks this standard.

## Reporting

Report a breach through https://ocalt.com/support, stating that the report concerns conduct.
